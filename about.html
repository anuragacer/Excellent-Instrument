Leading Calibration Laboratory in Northern India

EXCELLENT SERVICES HOUSE OF TESTING AND CALIBRATION is an independent facility established in 2008. Over nearly two decades, we have evolved into a trusted benchmark for quality calibration, repair, and technical up-gradation of measuring instruments.

"One Stop Shopping" — This is what our customers want, what they need, and what we provide. We listen to our partners and make every effort to satisfy their exact technical and regulatory business requirements.

Message from the Leadership
